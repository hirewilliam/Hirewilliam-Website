How fast can an AI engineer join my team?

Four steps, five business days, one engineer.

  1. Tell us you need an engineer

    One message in #recruitment, or an email to info@hirewilliam.com. You don’t need a job description, a budget line or a project plan. We take it from here.

  2. We study your business

    Your product, your stack, your competitors, the problems slowing you down and where you want to be in a year. Day 1 to 2.

  3. You get a 30-day blueprint

    What the engineer will build in their first month, in what order, and what it unlocks. You approve it before anyone starts. Day 3.

  4. Your engineer joins the team

    Matched to the blueprint, not pulled from a bench. They join your Slack, your repo and your standups. Day 4 to 5.
