How it works

Four steps, five business days, one engineer.

  1. Tell us you need an engineer

    One message in #talk-to-william. You don’t need a job description, a budget line or a project plan. We take it from here.

  2. We study your business

    Your product, your stack, your competitors, the problems slowing you down and where you want to be in a year. Day 1 to 2.

  3. You get a 30-day blueprint

    What the engineer will build in their first month, in what order, and what it unlocks. You approve it before anyone starts. Day 3.

  4. Your engineer joins the team

    Matched to the blueprint, not pulled from a bench. They join your Slack, your repo and your standups. Day 4 to 5.

Questions founders ask

Is this an employee or a contractor? A freelance engineer on a month-to-month arrangement through HireWilliam. No employment admin on your side.

What if it isn’t working? Tell us. We reassign. You’re never locked in past the current month.

Can I pick the engineer? We match to the blueprint and introduce them before day one. If it’s not the right fit, we go again before they start.

What kind of work do they do? Agents, automations, integrations, internal tools, data pipelines, AI features in your product. The blueprint spells out exactly what, for your business.

Do I need to know what I want built? No. “We need an engineer” is enough. The research and the blueprint are how we figure out the rest with you.
